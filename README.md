# B1 Garage Door Service — concept website

A single-page concept site for **B1 Garage Door Service LLC**, a family-run garage door
repair company in Arlington / DFW, TX (5.0★, 38 Google reviews). Built as a pitch preview
by Rashad — not the official B1 Garage Door Service website.

## The idea
The hero is **the garage door itself**. Press the remote: the door strains a few inches
up and slams back down — a snapped spring, car stuck inside. Tap "Send B1": a 25-minute
arrival window counts down (from a real review), a ~20-minute repair runs, the spring
turns from red to green, and the door rolls up to reveal the car inside.

## Build
- Single `index.html`, inline CSS + vanilla JS, no build step or dependencies.
- The door, spring and remote are drawn in CSS/SVG. Photos in `images/` are free Unsplash
  stock placeholders: `car-inside.jpg`, `home.jpg`, `door-closeup.jpg`, `tools.jpg`.
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed + Barlow (Google Fonts).
- Marked `noindex, nofollow`; footer labels it a concept preview.
